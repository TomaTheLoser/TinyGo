What's inside

PathFinder

Returns a Vector3 direction toward a target using Godot's NavigationAgent3D. Apply it to your Velocity and move. That's it.

Chunk

Loads and unloads scenes based on player distance. Optional preloading instantiates the scene early so it's ready the moment the player enters range.
